Garden Areas

Flowering Plants

  • The garden is designed around flowering plants and pollinators.
  • Colorful flowers, butterflies, bees and other insects.
  • Visitors can observe the relationship between insects and poliators.

Aquatic

  • These are garden areas that feature water and plants that grow in/around it.
  • These areas attract wildlife such as frogs, birds, butterflies, and other insects.
  • These gardens provide a peaceful environment.

Tropical

  • Tall trees commonly found in tropical regions such as Africa.
  • Provide shelter and food for various types of wildlife.
  • Thrive in warm temperatures and plenty of sunlight.

Succelent

  • Succulents store water in their leaves, stems, or roots.
  • They have thick, fleshy leaves that help them retain water.
  • Common examples include aloe, agave, and many types of cactus.

WHY DO PLANT MATTER??

  • Provide oxygen.
  • Support life.
  • Provide habitats.
  • Help maintain ecosytems.
  • Provide food & resources.
  • Add beauty both indoors and outdoors.
