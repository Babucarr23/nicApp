About the Conservatory

Nicholas Conservatory & Gardens is a botanical attraction with tropical plants, gardens, and educational displays. The conservatory provides visitors with an opportunity to explore plant life, animal life and learn about nature.

Help is needed with taking care of our plant and animal life. Please reachout if you're looking to volunteer in our conservatory.
